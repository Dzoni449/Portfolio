About

A short introduction from the people who run the place.

“We wanted a place where the wine list was longer than the menu — and where the menu changed enough that regulars still had reasons to be curious.”

Sela opened in the autumn of 2019 in a corner of Dorćol that used to be a bookshop. We kept the shelves. We built a small kitchen behind what used to be the reading nook. And we started cooking for the fifteen people who would fit in the room comfortably.

The idea, then and now, was simple: small plates that get better as the room gets fuller, wines from producers whose names you may not know yet, and a bar staffed by people who actually want to talk to you about what's in your glass.

The menu turns over on Tuesdays. Sometimes it's a full rewrite, sometimes a dish stays because the room won't let it go. We buy from the same three farms most of the year, and from the green market when they let us down. We make bread with a friend who runs a bakery two streets over, and we bought a copy of every wine book Andrej the sommelier could find — most of them are still on the shelves, if you want to browse while you wait.

We are closed on Mondays. We do not take reservations larger than eight. We do not have a set menu, a tasting menu, or a corporate account. What we have is fifteen seats, a good bar, and time. If any of that sounds like your kind of evening, come by.

The people

Who runs the room

Chef · Kitchen

Milena Đorđević

Trained in Copenhagen, cooked in London for four years, came home in 2019 to open Sela. Writes the menu on Sundays and cooks it herself Tuesday through Saturday.

Wine · Bar

Andrej Petrović

Sommelier for a decade, mostly in Ljubljana. Chooses every bottle on our list. Will happily talk your ear off about Ribolla if given half a chance.

Host · Room

Ana Milojković

Runs the front of house, the reservations book, and most of the honest opinions in the room. If you called about your birthday, you spoke to Ana.
