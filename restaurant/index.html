On the room

Sela is a fifteen-seat wine bar and kitchen tucked into a quiet street in Dorćol. We opened in the autumn of 2019 with three ideas: an ever-changing menu of small plates, a list weighted toward natural producers, and no music too loud to talk over.

A note on the list

Ninety-two bottles, most of them made by people we've met.

Our wine list changes constantly — we buy small quantities from producers we trust, and when a bottle sells out, it's usually gone for good. Expect a lot of natural wine from Slovenia, Croatia, Austria, and the Republic of Georgia, plus a rotating handful of French classics for people who came for something familiar.

Not sure where to start? Ask the person behind the bar. That's what they're there for.
